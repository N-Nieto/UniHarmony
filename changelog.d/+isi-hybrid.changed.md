`IntraSiteInterpolation` covariates now only restrict which samples are interpolated (and under-sampled) together and preserve the covariate distribution of each class and site, instead of balancing classes inside every stratum; `global_max` with covariates no longer fills every stratum up to the global maximum. Every class must have at least two samples in every site (one for random over-sampling), otherwise an error is raised; regression bins with too few samples in a site are left as they are, with a warning. Each site and stratum gets its own random stream, and the constructor parameters are no longer modified by `fit_resample`.
