Fix `fit_transform` of `NeuroComBat` and `ComBatGAM` forwarding fit-only options (`var_epsilon`, `delta_epsilon`, `tau_2_epsilon`, `max_iter`, `df`, `degree`, `smooth_covariates_bounds`) to `transform`, which raised a `TypeError`. `BaseComBat.fit_transform` now matches arguments to the signatures of `fit` and `transform` by name, so every ComBat variant inherits the fix (the `CovBat` and `ComBatGAM` overrides were removed), and unknown arguments raise a `TypeError` naming them.
