`ref_site` for the ComBat-based models (`NeuroComBat`, `ComBatGAM`, `CovBat`; implemented in the shared base class): harmonize every site to the mean and variance of one reference site, which is left unchanged (`ref.batch` of R `sva::ComBat`, conformance with neuroCombat's `ref_batch` tested), or to the pooled mean and variance of several reference sites; `ref_sites_` attribute; `CovBat(mean_only=...)`; gallery example "ComBat with a reference site".
