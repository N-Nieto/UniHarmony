`make_multisite_classification` input validation: non-integer `n_sites`, `n_features` or `n_classes` (including floats and booleans), non-finite strengths, a class that is absent from every site, a site with fewer samples than its classes, and more classes than the `"linear"` generator can place for the number of features now raise informative errors instead of crashing deep inside NumPy or scikit-learn or returning a single-class dataset. A class with a non-zero proportion in a site always gets at least one sample there (it could be rounded to 0), and a warning is logged when a class has fewer than 6 samples in a site, as many methods need more. A signal strength other than 1 with `"moons"` or `"circles"`, which ignore it, now warns.
