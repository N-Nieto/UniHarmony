`make_multisite_classification` additions: covariate effects (`Covariate(effect=..., effect_shape=..., effect_features=...)`: linear, quadratic, sigmoid or any function, or a shift per category; the same in every site, so covariates whose distribution differs between sites confound the site effect, as in real data); `return_ground_truth=True` returns every component of the data (signal, covariate values and effects, noise, the data without site effect, the offset and factor of every site and feature) to score harmonization exactly; the `"combat"` site effect, the generative model of ComBat with the priors of its empirical Bayes step; `site_effect_features` to restrict the site effect to some features; `site_effect_heterogeneity`, a per-sample intensity of the site effect shared by all features of the sample (a site-specific covariance, the case CovBat addresses); the `"class_interaction"` site effect, which differs between classes; a `"tiv"` covariate preset (total intracranial volume) that depends on sex, and `Covariate(depends_on=...)` for a continuous covariate whose mean depends on a categorical one; missing values with `site_effect={"dropout": {"value": np.nan, "prob": p}}` and their positions in `ground_truth["dropout_mask"]`; `site_names` to label the sites; `as_frame=True` for a DataFrame and Series; a NumPy `Generator` as `random_state`.
