`IntraSiteInterpolation`: the parent search that assigns interpolated targets in regression is vectorised (one Gram matrix per cell instead of a nearest-neighbour search per synthetic sample); `effective_dimension` uses the trace and Frobenius norm of the Gram matrix instead of an eigendecomposition and estimates the participation ratio from at most 2000 samples (`max_samples`), which keeps it cheap for voxel-wise data. The warnings for unsafe amplification and for regression bins that cannot be balanced now explain that part of the site-target association remains, and the documentation describes how to tune hyper-parameters with ISI nested in the inner cross-validation, the limits of ISI (strong imbalance with many features, local models, missing target ranges) and the within-site permutation probe.
