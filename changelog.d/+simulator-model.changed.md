`make_multisite_classification` (the same `random_state` gives different data than in 0.0.5): it returns `X, y, sites`, or `X, y, sites, ground_truth` with `return_ground_truth=True`; the covariates are in `ground_truth["covariates"]` and `return_base_data` is replaced by `ground_truth["signal"]`. All strengths (site effect, noise, covariate effects) are in units of the pooled within-class SD of each feature, so a strength means the same thing for every signal (moons and circles have a reference SD of 0.3–0.7, which made their effects up to 3 times stronger) and does not grow with the class separation. Location offsets are drawn from U(-s/2, s/2) instead of U(0, s): they are centred and the pooled mean no longer drifts (with equal strengths the differences between sites keep the same distribution; with unequal strengths, sites with a larger strength are no longer systematically shifted in the positive direction). Noise is added before the site effect, and the scale effect multiplies the signal and the noise of a sample around its expected value but not its covariate effects, as in ComBat's model; the `"nonlinear"` effect also acts around that value. The signal and the site effect are each given as a dict: `signal={"linear": 1.0}` replaces `signal_type` and `signal_strength`, and `site_effect={"location": 3.0, "scale": 1.0}` replaces `site_effect_type`, `site_effect_strength`, `site_scale_strength` and `site_class_interaction` (now the `"class_interaction"` type); `"location+scale"` is removed (use `{"location": s, "scale": s}`). The `"dropout"` site effect takes `{"value": v, "prob": p}` instead of a strength: values are replaced by `v` (e.g. `np.nan` for missing values, previously always 0) with probability `p` (previously min(0.1 s, 1)), per feature of a site or, with `"per": "value"`, per value. `site_effect_homogeneous` is replaced by `site_effect_heterogeneity` (the old per-sample jitter was independent across features and acted like extra noise). `Covariate(x_correlation=...)` and the signal name `"make_gaussian_quantiles"` are removed. The `"age"` and `"sex"` covariate presets now affect the features.
